# Tone of Voice — Acme Outbound

## 10 principles

1. **Specific over general.** "I saw you hired 3 SDRs in March" beats "I see you're scaling."
2. **Concrete over abstract.** Numbers, names, dates — not "many companies" or "in the industry."
3. **Question over claim.** Earn the right to assert; lead with curiosity.
4. **Short over comprehensive.** Cut every word that isn't load-bearing.
5. **Founder voice over agency voice.** Like a peer, not a vendor.
6. **One ask, not three.** A reader who has to choose says no.
7. **No flattery.** "Great company" / "impressive growth" trigger an instant skim.
8. **Soft CTAs over hard CTAs.** "Worth a 15-min look?" beats "Book a demo."
9. **Honest about what we don't know.** "I don't know if this is a fit, but…" outperforms confident assumption.
10. **Plain English.** No jargon, no buzzwords, no acronyms without context.

## Banned phrases

- "Hope this finds you well"
- "Just checking in"
- "Quick question"
- "Synergy" / "synergize"
- "Touch base"
- "Circle back"
- "Game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "World-class"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Solutions" (as a noun for product)
- Any sentence starting with "I came across your profile…"

## Channel rules

- **Cold email:** Under 80 words for E1. No images. No tracking pixels in personalized sends.
- **LinkedIn DM:** Under 50 words. Reference something they actually posted, not their bio.
- **LinkedIn comment:** Add a perspective, not a compliment. Never tag the team.

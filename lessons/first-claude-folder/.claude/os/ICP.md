# ICP — Acme Outbound (locked 2026-04-01)

## Firmographics

| Gate | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Stage | Post-PMF, paying customers, not pre-revenue |
| ARR band | $1–8M (sweet spot $2–4M) |
| Headcount | 5–25 |
| Vertical | B2B SaaS |
| GTM headcount | 0–1 dedicated GTM hires |
| Owner | Founder still owns sales and outbound |

## Anti-ICP — do not pursue

- Pre-PMF or pre-revenue
- $10M+ ARR (their problem isn't ours to solve)
- Agencies and consultancies (different buying motion)
- Enterprise sales-led companies with full SDR teams
- E-commerce, services, marketplaces

## Defining pain

> *Founder bandwidth caps revenue growth.* Not "we need more leads" — "I'm the bottleneck and I can't keep doing this."

## Section 3 — Verbatim ICP language to mirror

Use these phrases when writing outbound:
- "stuck in the founder-led sales loop"
- "outbound that doesn't fall apart when I take a week off"
- "I'm doing all of it and none of it well"
- "we have leads, we just can't follow up fast enough"

## Lead scoring

| Score | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| HOT | Hits 5/5 firmographic gates + clear trigger event | Personal email from founder, 24h response |
| WARM | 4/5 gates + thin signal | Standard sequence |
| COOL | 3/5 gates, no fresh signal | Nurture only — no direct outreach |

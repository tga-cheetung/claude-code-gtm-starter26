name: No emojis in cold email copy
description: User strips emojis from every cold email draft — including subject lines and PS lines
type: feedback
---

Never include emojis in cold email drafts, subject lines, LinkedIn DMs, or any client-facing outbound copy.

**Why:** User has tested with and without — emoji-laden subject lines tank reply rate in their ICP (B2B SaaS founders, $1–8M ARR). They review every draft before send and emojis are an instant rewrite trigger.

**How to apply:** Self-edit before returning any draft. If the user explicitly asks for an emoji (e.g. for an internal Slack message), follow the request — but never volunteer them in outbound contexts.
